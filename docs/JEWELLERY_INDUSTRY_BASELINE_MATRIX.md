## 1. Operating Principle & Mandate

> **"MODERNIZE THE IMPLEMENTATION. DO NOT REMOVE THE CAPABILITY."**
>
> The capabilities in this catalogue are not optional reference ideas. They represent the **mandatory functional depth** expected of a mature jewellery manufacturing and accounting ERP.
>
> Where legacy systems used 200 disconnected Windows forms, Ornexa consolidates them into **Universal Shared Engines** (*Party 360, Masters, Universal Transactions, Manufacturing Book, Tag Registry, Universal Reporting, Customization, Documents, and Migration*). However, **every individual business view, calculation, report, and ledger effect must exist and be fully operational**.
