### Core Commercial Policy Summary
1. **ONE PLATFORM. MULTIPLE LICENSABLE JEWELLERY BUSINESS CAPABILITIES.** Ornexa technically supports Manufacturing, Wholesale, and Retail from one maintainable architecture. But the tenant operates only the business capabilities included in its purchased license.
2. **BUSINESS MODE IS A LICENSED PRODUCT, NOT A FREE SETTINGS TOGGLE.** A tenant cannot freely switch from Manufacturing to Wholesale from regular settings without purchasing the corresponding commercial add-on.
3. **ASSISTED SETUP WIZARDS.** The onboarding wizard assists the customer in configuring the licensed product.
4. **EXPANSION WITHOUT CODE FORKS OR DATA MIGRATIONS.** When an existing firm buys an additional capability, the expansion wizard reuses existing parties, stock, accounts, and history.
5. **WORDS ARE CONFIGURABLE; BUSINESS CAPABILITY IS LICENSED.** Terminology customization is free within the licensed environment; unlocking new operational modules requires entitlement tokens.
