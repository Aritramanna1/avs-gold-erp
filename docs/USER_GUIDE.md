# User Guide

Sign in with your named account and work only in the assigned branch/modules. Use Customers/Suppliers for parties, Orders and Job Cards for production demand, Worker/Manufacturing Books for custody, Billing for financial documents, and Our Gold Stock/Gold Vault for gold movement. Never maintain a separate manual gold balance inside another module.

Use screen search and filters before creating duplicates. Complete required fields, check weights/purity, and read success/error messages. Use only the Universal Print and Export controls. Coming Soon pages are visible planned modules, not operational workflows.

If the app reports corruption, a failed restore, unresolved sync conflict, or an incorrect gold balance, stop posting related transactions and contact the Super Owner. Do not reinstall or delete application data.
