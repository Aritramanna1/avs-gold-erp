| **2026-08-14** | **Centralize Document & Printing Engine with 7 templates & profiles** | The previous print experience suffered from modal clipping, missing fields, and rigid layouts. Printing requires professional, high-fidelity multi-profile output. | Printing, Invoicing, Job Cards, Vouchers | Adds `print_profiles`, `document_templates`, and `print_terms_conditions` tables | Clean migration; no impact on existing transaction data | Product Owner | `DOCUMENT_PRINTING_MASTER.md` |
| **2026-08-14** | **Enforce conditional document field rendering** | Blank or "Not Recorded" placeholders look unprofessional. Empty optional fields must be omitted cleanly while mandatory compliance fields remain enforced. | Print Engine, Document Templates, Invoicing | None | None | Product Owner | `DOCUMENT_PRINTING_MASTER.md` |
| **2026-08-14** | **Mandate Buyer/Seller State Code & Place of Supply** | Accurate GST compliance (CGST+SGST vs IGST) requires explicit 2-digit state codes rather than parsing free-text addresses. | Party 360, Billing, Invoices, GST Reports | Extends `parties` and `invoices` with `state_code` and `place_of_supply` | Simple backfill script based on existing state names | Product Owner | `BUSINESS_RULES.md`, `DATABASE_AND_SUPABASE_MASTER.md` |
| **2026-08-14** | **Upgrade Platform Owner to SaaS Operations Control Centre** | Platform administration requires complete SaaS control (Tenant 360, Plans, Meta Partner WABA ops, AI quotas, Storage, Support) in a dedicated responsive layout. | SaaS Admin, Platform Control, Billing, WhatsApp | Extends `organizations`, `waba_configurations`, and `platform_plans` | Backward-compatible extensions | Product Owner | `PLATFORM_OWNER_MASTER.md` |
| **2026-08-14** | **Unify Gold Custody into Material Movement Engine with 4 Books** | Prevents fragmented, disconnected accounting by using one material movement engine with specialized views: Worker Gold, Outside Gold, Mina, and Polish Books. | Workshop, Custody, Job Cards, Outside Work | Unifies `worker_returns`, `outside_work_orders`, `mina_process_records`, `polish_process_records` | Consolidates legacy lot/batch records into unified movement ledger | Product Owner | `WORKFLOW_MASTER.md` |
| **2026-08-14** | **Implement Code Slicing & Fast Startup Architecture** | Monolithic JS bundles and loading all portals at startup degraded mobile performance. Applications must slice into Shared Core, Lazy Business Slices, and Isolated Portals. | App Shell, Router, Bundler, Performance | None | None | Product Owner | `PRODUCT_MASTER.md`, `RELEASE_READINESS.md` |
| **2026-08-14** | **Strict AI Safety Invariant: AI never calculates accounting** | LLMs are non-deterministic and must never be the source of truth for gold weight or financial ledgers. AI is limited to structured query routing and presentation. | AI Assistant, Voice Engine, RBAC | Adds `ai_assistant_sessions` and safe ERP tool catalog | None | Product Owner | `ASSISTANT_MASTER.md` |
| **2026-08-14** | **Server-side Central Email Service & Secure Password Reset** | Client-side email sending is insecure and unreliable. Centralized email service with single-use tokens prevents account enumeration and improves deliverability. | Auth, Email, Security, Communications | Adds `email_outbox` table and Supabase Edge Function email router | None | Product Owner | `COMMUNICATION_MASTER.md` |
| **2026-08-13** | **Supabase online-only is the sole authoritative architecture** | Deprecated all legacy local SQLite and offline-first sync architectures. Stack is strictly Supabase PostgreSQL + Auth + RLS + R2 Storage. | Architecture, Data Loader, Sync | Retained all historical business data; deprecated local SQLite tables | Migration to Supabase-backed schemas completed | Product Owner | `DATABASE_AND_SUPABASE_MASTER.md` |
| **2026-08-13** | **B2B Jewellery Manufacturing direction is authoritative** | Ornexa is fundamentally a workshop-first manufacturing ERP with job cards and gold custody, not a generic retail POS. | All core modules | Preserves dual metal/cash accounting and job work tracking | None | Product Owner | `PRODUCT_MASTER.md` |
