# Ornexa Master Product Spec

## Foundation

Tenant/company, branch/site, warehouse, users, roles, permissions, currencies, numbering, settings, page configuration, audit, backup/restore, subscription, licensing, and support control plane.

Ornexa must be one centralized jewellery business system, not disconnected modules. Modules are business views over shared authoritative foundations: one party system, one user/role system, one firm/branch model, one metal engine, one transaction foundation, one inventory foundation, one manufacturing graph, one accounting foundation, one document engine, one storage architecture, one notification/follow-up engine, one audit system, one reporting foundation, and one assistant tool layer.

## Commercial

Canonical party master with role profiles for customer, supplier, job worker/karigar, retailer, wholesaler, sales partner, billing party, employee, carrier/aangadiya, and branch/site. Quotations, orders, purchases, sales, returns, invoices, payments, reminders, settlement, and outstanding management.

## Product And Material

Items, categories, variations, metals, purity/touch, rates, stones, diamonds, designs, images, tags, barcodes, QR, HUID, parcels, asset items, boxes, trays, and locations.

Metal calculations must be centralized. Purchase, manufacturing, karigar, billing, inventory, reports, and assistant features must consume shared gross/net/fine/touch/purity/rate/value logic and preserve historical calculation snapshots.

## Manufacturing

Process templates/graphs, production requirement, work order/job card, issue, WIP, receive, casting, melting, touch, alloy, labour, loss, waste, recovery, next process, QC/approval, rework, hallmark, finished tagging, and stock.

## Inventory And Movement

Raw metal, fine metal, finished jewellery, semi-finished/WIP, scrap, dust, recovery, diamonds, stones, components, branch transfer, warehouse transfer, in-transit, reservations, allocations, stock match, physical stock audit, ageing, reorder, and item transaction history.

## Finance And People

Cash, bank, expenses, investments, customer/supplier/karigar ledgers, payroll, labour, holidays, advances, bonuses, incentives, credit salary, payments, allocation, day close, period close, reconciliation, Tally/export-ready accounting.

## Intelligence And Outputs

Dashboard, day book, reports, valuation, profit, turnover, balance views, PDF, Excel, print, thermal, barcode, QR, WhatsApp, email, portals, automation, assistant, and observability.

Checklist Design's Web App checklist is an additional release quality gate. Relevant web-app checks are maintained in `docs/ORNEXA_WEBAPP_COMPLETENESS_CHECKLIST.md`; production readiness requires applicable P0/P1 checks to pass with evidence.
