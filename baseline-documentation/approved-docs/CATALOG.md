# Catalog

Catalog is the reusable jewellery-manufacturing design library at `/catalog`.

## Capabilities

- configurable categories, tags, default purity, and expected weight range from Settings;
- internal, customer-reference, supplier-reference, and order-derived designs;
- searchable and filterable design cards with summary counts;
- customer association for customer-reference designs;
- encrypted design photo, customer reference, and technical drawing attachments;
- CSV output through the Universal Export Engine utility;
- validated gross/net weight entry using milligram storage and gram display;
- localized create, list, detail, edit, and delete workflows.

Catalog stores design references only. It does not maintain gold balances or stock quantities. Any gold movement remains owned by Gold Vault and its operational modules.
