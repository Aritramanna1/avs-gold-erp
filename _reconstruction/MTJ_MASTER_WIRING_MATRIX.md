## 3. Wiring Audit Verdict
- **No Orphan Features**: Every UI trigger writes directly to its domain store and persists to Supabase `app_settings` or dedicated PostgreSQL tables.
- **Strict Accounting Propagation**: Transactions immediately update Customer Dual Ledgers or Karigar Custody Books, which feed Dar Rojmel day books and the Universal Print Engine.
- **Zero Parallel / Fake Calculations**: Screen previews, PDFs, emails, and WhatsApp messages derive from the same underlying domain models.
