Dual-Currency Mathematics: Fine Gold (g) & Cash (₹) Calculation Rules

Canonical Formulas

1. Net Weight & Fine Gold Conversion Core Standard

Net weight strictly deducts stones, enamel, and wax weight. Fine gold is computed at 24K (1000‰ purity basis).

Net (mg) = Gross Weight (mg) - Less Weight (mg)
Fine Gold (mg) = [Net (mg) × Purity (‰)] ÷ 1000
22K (91.6%) Example: 10,000mg Net × 916 ÷ 1000 = 9,160mg Fine Gold

2. Workshop Wastage & Bench Loss Karigar Hisab

Workshop allows contracted wastage percentage on gross gold issue. Bench balance determines worker liability.

Allowed Wastage (mg) = Gross Issued × Wastage%
Worker Balance = Fine Issued - (Finished Fine + Scrap Fine + Allowed Wastage)
Loss Recovery: Over-loss is settled in pure 24K gold or cash at current Bhav

3. Invoicing, Making Charges & GST Billing Engine

Making charges apply per gram or percentage. GST (3%) applies to total of (Gold Value + Making + Stone Charges).

Gold Value (₹) = [Net Weight (g) × Today Bhav (₹/g)]
Taxable Amount = Gold Value + Making Charges + Stones + Hallmark Fee
CGST (1.5%) + SGST (1.5%) = Taxable Amount × 3%

4. Old Gold Exchange & Valuation Customer Intake

Customer exchange jewellery is melted/tested for purity. Purity difference is adjusted into pure gold deposit.

Old Gold Value (₹) = Net Old Weight × Purity% × Old Gold Purchase Rate
Exchange Offset: Invoice Total Payable = Invoice Amount - Old Gold Credit (₹)
Or Deposited to Customer Gold Ledger as pure 24K credit grams

5. Dual Ledger Account Balance Invariant World-First Audit

Customer balance is maintained simultaneously in Cash (₹) and Pure Gold (mg). No automatic mixing without transaction.

Customer Cash Due (₹) = Σ Invoices(Balance) - Σ Cash Receipts(Paise)
Customer Gold Credit (mg) = Σ Pure Gold Deposits(mg) - Σ Gold Applied(mg)
Zero Silent Conversion: Metal debt requires explicit Rate Lock / Sale document

6. Outside Work & Subcontracting Specialist Services

Enameling (Meenakari), CAD Casting, Diamond Setting, and Laser Engraving outsourced to third-party vendors.

Outside Worker Custody = Metal Issued (g) - Metal Returned (g)
Service Charge Payable = Pieces × Per-Piece Labour OR Grams × Rate/g
Rollup: Accumulated directly into final item cost & workshop statement

The 10 Canonical Reporting Families: Source Data → Transformation → Output Formats

Executive & Operational Reports

1. Daily Gold Flow Report reports.daily-gold-flow

Daily physical metal movement across Vault, Counter, Workshop, and Melting.

Sources: worker_gold_book, invoices, old_gold, vault_safes

Outputs: Opening Metal (g) + Inflows (Purchases/Old Gold) - Outflows (Sales/Karigar) = Closing Metal.

2. Metal Position & Reserve Report reports.metal-position

Complete firm-wide valuation of metal in stock, karigar custody, and customer accounts.

Sources: tag_registry, worker_gold_book, customer_gold_ledger

Outputs: 24K, 22K, 18K breakdown, Pure equivalent, Market value at live Bhav.

3. Sales & Tax Register (GSTR-1) reports.sales-register

Full statutory GST sales register for tax filing and chartered accountant audits.

Sources: invoices, credit_notes, app_settings

Outputs: HSN 7113 breakdown, Taxable, CGST, SGST, IGST, E-Way Bill details.

4. Workshop WIP & Karigar Custody reports.outside-work

Real-time bench accountability tracking gold with each master craftsman.

Sources: job_cards, worker_returns, worker_gold_book

Outputs: Karigar wise gold issued, finished jewellery returned, net pending liability.

5. Total Profit & Loss Statement reports.total-profit

Real-time margin intelligence combining metal appreciation and making revenue.

Sources: invoices, expenses, old_gold, bhav_history

Outputs: Metal Margins + Making Profit + Stone Profit - Operating Expenses = Net Profit.

6. Customer Account Ledger Statement reports.ledger-statement

Formal customer dual statement showing both ₹ and Pure Gold transactions.

Sources: invoices, payments, customer_gold_ledger

Outputs: High-fidelity PDF statement with instant WhatsApp share deep link.

7. Daily Close & Daybook Register reports.daybook

End-of-day reconciliation across Cash in drawer, Bank UPI, and Gold in vault.

Sources: payments, drawings, expenses, vault_safes

Outputs: Physical cash verification, variance report, CEO day-end signoff.

8. Stock Audit & Tag Discrepancy reports.stock-audit

Physical tray-by-tray scanning audit against system inventory register.

Sources: tag_registry, stock_audit_sessions

Outputs: Scanned tags vs Missing tags vs Unregistered items with weight diffs.

9. Outside Worker & Specialist Charges reports.outside-work

Labour bills and metal reconciliation for Hallmarking centers, Meenakari, and CAD.

Sources: outside_work_charges, job_cards

Outputs: Pending vendor bills, service history, item cost attribution.

10. Full ERP Audit & Compliance Trail reports.erp-audit

Tamper-evident log of every record creation, status change, deletion, and email dispatch.

Sources: audit_log, communication_audit_log

Outputs: Actor, IP, Device, Before/After state, Timestamp, Reference error IDs.
