**Date:** 2026-08-12

This matrix benchmarks **APPIT Jewel ERP** vs. **Jwelly ERP** vs. **Ornexa (AVS Gold ERP)** across core functionality.

---

## 2. Competitive Positioning

- **Jwelly ERP**: Strong legacy on-premise jewel ERP with deep localized features, but lacks modern cloud-native features, real-time sync, and Karigar portals.
- **APPIT Jewel ERP**: Sleek cloud-native POS system with excellent visual dashboards, appraiser valuation rules, and branch management. However, its backend ledger system is not a true double-entry cash+metal accounting engine.
- **Ornexa (AVS Gold ERP)**: Positioned as the most advanced cloud ERP in the market by merging APPIT's beautiful aesthetics and valuation workflows with Jwelly's core business compliance, alongside a true **double-entry dual ledger (Cash + Gold)** backend and a self-service **Karigar OTP Portal**.
